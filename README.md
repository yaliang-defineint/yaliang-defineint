<summary><b>Behind the Username</b></summary>
<br>

* **`yaliang`**  
  This originally started as a random nickname (雅量) typed out in a game. It later became widely used by my friends and classmates because of the junior high school textbook lesson "像棋盤，像稿紙，像綠豆糕" (《雅量》 - 宋晶宜).
* **`defineint`**  
  Rooted in early days of competitive programming with C++. While writing boilerplates full of `#define` statements, macro-defining `int` was an unconventional irony. It stands as a reminder of where it all started.
</details>
